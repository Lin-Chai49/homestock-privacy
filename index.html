1. 这份说明对应哪一版

适用应用是悦家库存 HomeStock，包名 com.chailin.homestock，versionName 1.0.0，versionCode 2。最低系统为 Android 7.0（minSdk 24），目标系统为 targetSdk 35。著作权人是柴琳。本页地址是 https://lin-chai49.github.io/homestock-privacy/。

政策自 2026-05-13 起适用。2026-11-01 按这一版代码重写了正文，去掉原先写得过满、应用里并没有的说法。

2. “不收集”是什么意思

这里的“不收集”是指开发者收不到。应用没有账号，没有登录，也没有把数据库上传到服务器。你在屏幕上输入的内容仍然会留在这台手机的 HomeStockDB 里。导出 JSON 之后，那个文件在你保存它的地方，应用不再管它。

应用“关于”里写着“所有数据本地存储，零数据采集，无网络请求”。这句话指的是开发者收不到、应用代码不去请求网络。它不表示手机上没有一份本地数据库。

3. 留在手机里的数据库

第一次打开时，应用创建 IndexedDB 数据库 HomeStockDB，版本 1。同一来源下只有这个应用的 WebView 能读写它。其他应用读不到这份库。库里有六个对象存储：

存储里面有什么
items一件物资一条。见下一节字段。
categories预置 8 个类别：食品、药品、日用品、清洁用品、调味品、罐头、茶饮、其他。设置页只列出它们。这一版的屏幕不能新增或删除类别。导入备份可以整表替换。
locations预置 12 个存放位置，例如冷藏、冷冻、药箱、卫生间柜。这是房间和柜子的名字，不是 GPS。这一版的屏幕不能新增或删除位置。导入备份可以整表替换。
consumptions消耗记录：物品、数量、场景、备注、时间。场景是你选的早餐、午餐、日常等标签，不是传感器。
shoppingList购物清单：名称、类别、数量、估计价格、建议去处的文字、来源、是否买完。
settings一条键为 app 的设置。见第 4 节。

如果你把一件东西记在“药品”下面，药名、效期、数量和备注都在这台手机的 items 里。开发者收不到，应用也不据此做诊断。

4. 你会填进设置里的内容

引导页要求填写昵称，2 到 20 个字符，填完才能进入首页。昵称会显示在首页和“更多”页。设置里还可以保存这些内容，全部在 settings 那一条记录里：

字段你看到的
昵称必填才能离开引导页。最长 20 个字符。
家庭角色五个固定选项之一，默认“家庭主管”。
家庭人数数字，默认 3，范围 1 到 20。
家庭住址选填，最长 50 个字符。这是你键入的文字，不是定位。应用不声明定位权限，也不读 GPS。
显示主题（家居暖色、深色模式、清新自然）、列表样式、默认排序、临期阈值天数、最低库存比率。
提醒开关见第 6 节。只保存，不发送。
quietHours随默认设置写成 22:00 到 07:00。设置页不能改这两个钟点。因为没有提醒任务，这两个钟点也不会被用到。
onboardedAt完成引导的本机时间戳。

设置里还有字号和日期格式的默认值。这一版的设置屏幕不编辑它们。

5. 物品、消耗和购物清单

入库屏幕让你填写：名称、类别、品牌、规格、数量、单位、最低库存、购买日期、到期日期、购买金额、备注、存放位置。购买金额只用于本机上的花费图表，不是支付。

每条物品记录里还有 barcode 和 imageUrl 两个空字段。这一版的入库屏幕不填写它们。应用不打开相机，不打开相册，也不扫码。不要把这两个字段理解成已经在读你的照片。

消耗一条记录时，应用记下物品名称、数量、你选的场景和可选备注，以及当时的时间。购物清单里的“建议去处”是写死在应用里的类别标签，例如食品对应“盒马生鲜”，日用品对应“京东超市”，药品对应“京东大药房”，茶饮对应“永辉超市”。这不是在查询这些商店，也没有打开它们的网站。

6. 提醒开关

设置页“通知与提醒”有七个开关：临期提醒、已过期提醒、库存不足提醒、购物清单更新、每周报告（周一上午）、月度总结（每月初）、免打扰时段（22:00-07:00）。点“保存修改”后，它们写进 settings.notifications。

版本 1.0.0 不发送系统通知。没有定时任务去读这七个开关，首页也不读它们。应用不声明 POST_NOTIFICATIONS。免打扰时段因此也不会生效。开关留在数据库里，只是设置值。

7. 本机计算

临期分档、用近 30 天消耗估计还能用几天、是否放入购物清单、花费和浪费图表，都在这台手机上算。应用代码里没有 fetch、XHR、WebSocket 或上报接口。图表库不会为了这些图去下载地图或统计脚本。

8. 导出、导入、演示数据和清空

这些按钮都在“设置 → 数据管理”。

操作实际做什么
导出数据（JSON）在内存里拼一份 JSON，包含 schema（值为 HomeStockDB）、version、exportedAt，以及六个存储的全部行，含昵称和家庭住址。然后开始下载，文件名形如 homestock-backup-YYYY-MM-DD-HHmm.json。应用不上传这个文件。你若把它存进“下载”或其他应用，那份副本不受本应用卸载的影响。
导入备份你从系统文件选择器选一个 JSON。schema 不是 HomeStockDB 就停止，并提示格式不正确。通过后，应用先清空六个存储，再写入文件。这会覆盖物品，也会覆盖类别、位置和设置，包括昵称与住址。导入前有一次确认。
加载演示数据在现有数据后面追加大约 20 件示例物品、对应的消耗记录和几条购物清单。不先删除你已有的物品。确认框里的“追加”和代码一致。
清空全部数据连续确认两次后，删除物品、消耗记录和购物清单。保留类别、位置和设置。昵称、家庭角色、家庭人数和家庭住址还在。

导出的 JSON 在 1.0.0 里不加密。谁能打开那个文件，谁就能读到物资清单和你填过的住址。

9. 权限和系统备份

清单只声明一项权限：android.permission.INTERNET。Capacitor 用 https 方案把随包的页面交给 WebView，这项权限是为了那次本机加载。应用同时声明 usesCleartextTraffic="false"，并且不允许混合内容。运行时没有明文请求，也没有应用自己发出的网络请求。

清单不声明相机、麦克风、定位、电话、短信、通讯录、外部存储读写、通知或生物识别。应用代码不读剪贴板。

android:allowBackup="false"，android:fullBackupContent="false"。自定义 dataExtractionRules 把 root、database、file、sharedpref、external 从云备份和换机传输里都排除。系统的自动云备份不会带走 HomeStockDB。

工程里有一个不导出的 FileProvider，authority 是 com.chailin.homestock.fileprovider。这一版的屏幕不用它把文件交给别的应用。导出走的是上面的 JSON 下载。

卸载应用会清掉这个应用的 WebView 存储，包括 HomeStockDB。已经下载到别处的 JSON 不会跟着消失。

10. 随包带上的库

下面这些库在构建时打进安装包，应用没有给它们配账号或统计地址：Vue 3、Vue Router、Pinia、idb、ECharts、dayjs、Capacitor 6。没有广告 SDK、统计 SDK、推送 SDK、崩溃收集或社交分享 SDK。

11. 儿童

悦家库存是给建立这份家庭账的人用的物资工具，不是儿童服务，也没有账号。开发者收不到手机里记下的昵称、住址或药品名称。本政策不把应用说成面向儿童，也不宣称某个年龄分级。

12. 安全边界

本地数据的安全取决于这台手机的锁屏和系统加密。版本 1.0.0 没有加密备份，没有单独的隐私弹窗，设置里的“关于”也没有更新日志。要看这份说明，打开本页。

13. 政策变更

如果以后的版本开始发送通知、读取照片，或把数据传到开发者的服务器，会先改本页，并改页首的日期。版本 1.0.0 不会在启动时弹出确认。关于页也没有政策日志。

HomeStock privacy policy

HomeStock (悦家库存) version 1.0.0, package com.chailin.homestock, versionCode 2. Developer: 柴琳. This page applies from 2026-05-13 and was rewritten on 2026-11-01 to match that build. The minimum system is Android 7.0 (minSdk 24). targetSdk is 35.

“Not collected” means the developer does not receive it. The app has no account and no login. What you type stays in the on-device database HomeStockDB unless you export a file.

Database

On first launch the app creates IndexedDB HomeStockDB, version 1. Only this app’s WebView origin can read it. The six stores are items, categories, locations, consumptions, shoppingList, and settings.

The entry form stores a name, category, brand, specification, quantity, unit, minimum stock, purchase date, expiry date, price, note, and a storage place. Price is used only for charts on the device. It is not a payment. Each item row also has empty barcode and imageUrl fields. This version’s form does not fill them. The app does not open the camera, does not open the photo library, and does not scan a code.

Eight categories ship with the app: food, medicine, daily goods, cleaning, condiments, canned goods, tea and drinks, and other. Twelve places ship with it, such as the fridge and the medicine box. Those are room labels, not GPS. This version’s screens do not add or delete a category or a place. Import can replace both tables. If you file something under medicine, the name and expiry stay on the device. The developer does not receive them and the app does not diagnose anything.

A consumption row stores the item name, amount, a scene you pick (breakfast, lunch, daily, and similar labels), an optional note, and the time. A shopping row can store a suggested place name. Those names are fixed labels in the app: food maps to 盒马生鲜, daily goods to 京东超市, medicine to 京东大药房, tea and drinks to 永辉超市. The app does not contact those stores.

What settings store

The first-run screen requires a nickname of 2 to 20 characters. The home screen and the More screen show it. Settings can also store a household role (five fixed choices, default 家庭主管), a household size (default 3, range 1 to 20), and an optional address of up to 50 characters. The address is text you type. The app does not declare a location permission and does not read GPS. Theme, list layout, sort, expiry threshold, and minimum-stock ratio are stored in the same settings row, under the key app. onboardedAt is the on-device time when setup finished. Font size and date format have defaults; this version’s settings screen does not edit them.

Reminder switches

Settings shows seven switches: 临期提醒, 已过期提醒, 库存不足提醒, 购物清单更新, 每周报告（周一上午）, 月度总结（每月初）, and 免打扰时段（22:00-07:00）. Saving writes them into settings.notifications. Version 1.0.0 does not post a system notification. No scheduled job reads the switches, and the home screen does not read them either. The app does not declare POST_NOTIFICATIONS. The quiet-hours clock is stored as 22:00 to 07:00, the settings screen does not edit those two times, and nothing applies them because there is no reminder job.

Export, import, demo data, and clear

All four actions are under Settings, then 数据管理.

  • Export JSON. The app builds one document in memory. It includes schema (HomeStockDB), version, exportedAt, and every row of the six stores, including the nickname and address. It then starts a download named like homestock-backup-YYYY-MM-DD-HHmm.json. The app does not upload that file. A copy you keep in Downloads stays after you uninstall the app. The file is not encrypted.
  • Import. You pick a JSON file. If schema is not HomeStockDB, import stops. If it matches, the app clears all six stores and writes the file. That replaces items, categories, locations, and settings, including the nickname and address. One confirmation comes first.
  • Demo data. The app appends about 20 sample items, their consumption rows, and a few shopping rows. It does not delete your existing items first.
  • Clear all. After two confirmations, the app deletes items, consumption rows, and the shopping list. It keeps categories, locations, and settings. The nickname, role, household size, and address remain.

Permission and backup

The manifest declares one permission, android.permission.INTERNET. Capacitor loads the bundled page in the WebView on an https scheme, and that permission is what allows the local load. usesCleartextTraffic is false, and mixed content is off. The app source does not call fetch, XHR, WebSocket, or a beacon. The manifest does not declare the camera, microphone, location, phone, SMS, contacts, external storage, notifications, or biometrics. The app code does not read the clipboard.

android:allowBackup is false and android:fullBackupContent is false. dataExtractionRules exclude root, database, file, sharedpref, and external from both cloud backup and device transfer. Automatic cloud backup does not take HomeStockDB.

The project includes a FileProvider, authority com.chailin.homestock.fileprovider, and it is not exported. The screens in this version do not use it to hand a file to another app. Export is the JSON download above.

Uninstall removes the app’s WebView storage, including HomeStockDB. It does not remove a JSON file you already saved somewhere else.

Libraries, children, and the limit of this text

Vue 3, Vue Router, Pinia, idb, ECharts, dayjs, and Capacitor 6 are bundled in the install. The app does not point them at an account or an analytics endpoint. There is no ad, analytics, push, crash, or social SDK.

HomeStock is a household inventory tool for the person who sets it up. It is not a children’s service and it has no account. The developer does not receive the nickname, address, or medicine names stored on the phone. This policy does not assign an age rating.

Safety of the on-device data is the phone’s lock screen and encryption. Version 1.0.0 has no encrypted backup, no privacy popup at launch, and no changelog under About. This page is the notice. If a later version starts posting notifications, reading photos, or sending data to the developer, this page and the date at the top will change first. The 2026-05-13 text described reminders and an encrypted backup the app does not have. Those sentences are gone.

Version 1.0.0’s About screen and this page do not list an email address. After the app is on Google Play, use the listing’s feedback. Source of this page: github.com/Lin-Chai49/homestock-privacy.
